Services

Full-service technical production, or exactly the piece you're missing.

Hire us for one discipline or the whole show. Either way, everything is designed to the same standard: it has to work, live, with no second take.

Cue01

Audio Visual Production

Line array and point-source sound systems, LED screens and projection mapping, vision switching and camera coverage. We spec the rig to the room and the content, not the other way round, and every system ships with a tested backup path.

Cue02

Lighting & Staging Design

Stage design, rigging and structural, and lighting programmed to the run of show rather than a generic preset. We work from CAD through to focus, so what's drawn is what the room actually looks like.

Cue03

Creative Technology

Interactive installations, real-time generative visuals, show control and custom integrations, built the same way we built the Neon Flow piece on our homepage: brand-true, responsive, and genuinely interactive rather than a looping video.

Cue04

Live Streaming & Broadcast

Multi-camera direction, graphics overlays and redundant streaming infrastructure for hybrid events, product launches and internal broadcasts, with the same production values as the in-room show.

Cue05

Event Technical Production

The project management layer that holds a production together: budgets, site surveys, vendor coordination, run sheets and on-site technical direction, so the client has one number to call.

Cue06

Equipment & Crew Hire

Dry or crewed hire of AV, lighting and staging equipment for productions already under someone else's direction: our gear, our technicians, your show.

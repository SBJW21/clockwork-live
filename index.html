A straight line from first call to final cue.

Complex shows are solved one piece at a time. We break every production into clear stages with a named owner on each.

  1. 01

    Brief

    We start with the goal of the event, not the kit list, so the production serves the moment and protects the creative idea.

  2. 02

    Design

    CAD drawings, show flow and a budget you can hold us to, before a single cable goes near the venue.

  3. 03

    Build

    Our crew manages load-in, then rigs, tests and rehearses on-site, with redundancy built in everywhere it matters.

  4. 04

    Run

    We operate the show live and watch every signal path, so you can watch the room instead. Load-out is ours too.
